# Completed lab README template

Use this structure when documenting completed work. Replace every placeholder and confirm the evidence before adding a Validated badge. Planned pages remain Planned. See [repository maintenance](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

```markdown
# Lab NN — Descriptive task title

[AZ-104 badge] [Validated badge after evidence review]

[Portfolio] · [All labs] · [Evidence gallery]

## Outcome

State the demonstrated result and its most important limitation in two or three sentences.

## Business problem

Explain the requirement and the access or operational boundary being tested.

## Environment and decisions

Record resources, identities, role scopes, network paths and configuration choices.

## Work performed

Describe completed actions in execution order. Separate intermediate settings from final state.

## Validation results

Use a table: test, observed result, and evidence or limitation. Preserve failed tests and recovery.

## Troubleshooting and lessons

Record the symptom, corrective action and retest. Label an unconfirmed cause as unknown.

## Cleanup and costs

State what was removed, what remained, how cleanup was checked, and the cost review's date and scope.

## Evidence and limits

Include one representative screenshot and link the complete gallery. State what was not tested.

[Previous lab] · [All labs] · [Next lab]
```

The gallery starts with the actual screenshot count, a table linking every image, and any capture-specific qualifications. Do not add credentials, tokens, private keys, subscription IDs, personal billing details, or unreviewed screenshots.
